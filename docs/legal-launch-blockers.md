# Legal rollout: release decisions, not a compliance certificate

Research date: 20 September 2026. Scope: public user-generated requests, comments and media; voluntary direct crypto/SBP support; no platform commission or custody represented. Interface locale is not residence, nationality, tax residence or jurisdiction.

## Must resolve with the actual operator before describing an international launch as legally ready

1. **Identify the operator.** Verify its legal name, service/contact address, establishment, legal form, registration details where required, responsible privacy contact and actual operational ownership. Use a verified project-domain contact channel, never a personal email address. A mailbox alone is not proof of any legal identity, DPO appointment, EU representative or DMCA designation.
2. **Operational reporting, especially intimate imagery.** Assign a monitored inbox and backup human coverage, timestamp incoming notices, confirm receipt, review lawful requests, remove content and known identical copies, explain decisions and handle appeals. The US TAKE IT DOWN Act has applied since 19 May 2026: covered platforms must act as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours after valid notice. A generated email draft does not submit anything, guarantee delivery, staff the inbox, search duplicates or meet that duty. Verify actual removal/media purge tools and escalation. DSA notice acknowledgement, reasoned decisions and contact/representation duties need an operational owner too.
3. **Data map and enforceable lifecycle.** Verify production regions, subprocessors, signed DPAs, international-transfer mechanism and supplementary assessment for Clerk, Supabase, Vercel, Telegram and mail. Document each retained category, purpose/basis, deletion/anonymisation schedule, backup expiry, logs and incident runbook. Do not invent a retention promise or say a browser preference deletes account data.
4. **Russia: stop short of clearance.** No Russian-localised database or compliant cross-border collection path was evidenced. Russian-language and SBP features make territorial review particularly important. Obtain current advice on 152-FZ arts 12/18(5)/22, consent/public dissemination, notices and Russian primary storage before collecting covered data. Also verify the complete new 4 August 2026 282-FZ text, transitional provisions and practical treatment of direct gifts, wallet instructions and advertising. The official publication was located but could not be fully retrieved in this research session; old 259-FZ summaries alone are not current clearance.
5. **Payment/crypto/charitable solicitation classification.** Map every transaction-building/verification/deep-link feature. No custody and no commission are facts, not universal exemptions. Singapore's expanded PSA covers some facilitation without possession. Check EU MiCA/payment/AML scope, US FinCEN and state money transmission/charity rules, Malaysian SC/BNM rules, and Russia's current regime. Do not claim registered charity status, tax deductibility, financial authorisation or guaranteed lawful access.
6. **Country-specific completion.** The EU matrix identifies all 27 authorities and the common EU baseline. Local ePrivacy implementation, mandatory contract-language/imprint/charity requirements remain country-by-country legal review items, not completed national legal opinions. US threshold examples are not a 50-state certification; sensitive/health data, youth rules and small-business exceptions need actual scope assessment.
7. **Malaysia.** Supply the section 7 notice in Bahasa Malaysia and English, including contact, purposes/sources, recipients, rights, choices and mandatory/optional consequences. The supplemental notice implements the bilingual presentation; operator facts, transfer safeguards, retention decisions, any DPO/registration obligation, security and breach operations remain unresolved.
8. **Age/sensitive content.** User stories can expose health/financial information or children. Do not infer age verification or parental-consent functionality from rules in terms. Decide audience eligibility and enforce it; prevent unnecessary sensitive documents and third-party data; assess applicable youth/data rules before targeted expansion.

## Implemented surface vs operations

The new `/legal` pages provide factual service rules, a data-use inventory, a storage notice, independently selectable country guidance, regulator links and a no-login email-draft workflow for rights, illegal content, intimate imagery, copyright and appeals. They deliberately do not assert universal compliance or fabricate a controller. Optional view counting is subject to the privacy-control implementation and needs runtime verification before its notice is considered accurate. A legal notice never waives mandatory consumer, privacy or reporting rights.

No production migration, commit or deployment is performed by the legal subtask.
