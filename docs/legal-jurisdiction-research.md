# Regional legal implementation — 7 October 2026

This is a source-backed implementation record, not a legal opinion or assurance against lawsuits. The actual operator, establishment, targeting, data flows and payment features determine applicability. No operator identity, address, licence, DPO or representative was invented.

## European Union

The implementation covers the common GDPR privacy-notice and rights baseline, ePrivacy storage choice, and DSA access to content reports. Privacy/cookie/terms/report pages are accessible without an account. Optional unique-view counting requires prior opt-in everywhere; refusal is equally accessible; withdrawal clears the browser ID. Necessary account, requested preference and transfer-recovery storage is explained separately. A language or IP estimate cannot waive rights.

Primary sources: [GDPR, notably arts 3, 6, 12–22, 27, 28, 32–34 and 44–49](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng); [ePrivacy Directive art 5(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ENG/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32002L0058); [DSA, notably arts 11–17 and conditional platform duties](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj/eng); [EDPB national authorities](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/our-members_en).

National regulator guidance reviewed includes [Ireland DPC](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/dpc-guidance/guidance-cookies-and-other-tracking-technologies), [France CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cookies-et-autres-traceurs/regles/cookies), [Spain AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies.pdf), and [Italy Garante](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/home/docweb/-/docweb-display/docweb/9677876). The controls apply a conservative opt-in baseline instead of relying on a national analytics exemption.

## United States

The pages disclose collection/use, public activity, providers and choices; provide privacy requests and appeals; explain voluntary transfers and fees; and provide a no-login route for content reports. Global Privacy Control forces necessary-only optional storage. No sale/share/advertising integration is introduced by this rollout.

Primary sources: [California DOJ CCPA](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa), [2026 CCPA statute from CPPA](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute_eff_20260101.pdf), and [FTC TAKE IT DOWN guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act).

State-law scope and thresholds vary and require the operator's real circumstances; small size alone is not a complete exemption analysis. The public reporting workflow collects a URL, explanation, contact, requested action, good-faith statement and, for intimate imagery/copyright, name and electronic signature. It explicitly opens an email draft; the reporter must send it. Covered-platform intimate-imagery removal duties and identical-copy efforts require human coverage and removal operations. The page does not claim a verified 48-hour service-level guarantee or registered DMCA agent.

## Russia

The Russian notice discloses that Russian-localised collection/storage is unverified and that local personal-data and cross-border requirements need an operator-specific assessment. Consent wording is not a substitute for infrastructure obligations. Supabase's project is configured in `eu-central-1`; no Russian primary database was introduced by this rollout.

Primary current digital-currency source located: [Bank of Russia legal acts](https://www.cbr.ru/develop/acts/), which refers to Federal Law 282-FZ of 4 August 2026. The full current statutory text, commencement/transitional details and classification of this precise gift/transfer flow were not verified; do not claim permission based on old 259-FZ summaries. Consult the [official legal portal](https://www.pravo.gov.ru/) and [Roskomnadzor](https://rkn.gov.ru/) for 152-FZ localisation, notices, consent/public disclosure and cross-border requirements. These remain release prerequisites for a claim of Russian compliance, not features accomplished by translation.

## Singapore

The Singapore notice explains PDPA rights/contact/overseas transfers and the absence of a claimed payment licence or charity registration.

Primary sources: [PDPC data-protection obligations](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/the-legislation/personal-data-protection-act/data-protection-obligations); [Payment Services Act, first schedule](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PSA2019); [Charity Portal fundraiser duties](https://www.charities.gov.sg/Pages/Fund-Raising/Fund-Raisers-Duties-and-Obligations.aspx); [scope of fundraising appeals](https://www.charities.gov.sg/Pages/Fund-Raising/About-Fund-Raisers.aspx).

Where applicable, an actual DPO/public business contact, lawful processing, protection/retention controls and comparable overseas protection are needed. Arranging digital-token transmission may fall within the expanded PSA without possession of tokens. Charitable appeals targeting Singapore require their own scope/disclosure/permit analysis. No custody/no commission is not universal clearance.

## Malaysia

The main privacy page includes an additional Bahasa Malaysia **and English** notice. It explains data categories/sources/purposes, public/restricted disclosures, service providers, voluntary and necessary inputs, consequences of not supplying account data, choices, contact and access/correction/withdrawal requests. These two legal-notice languages are independent from the five interface languages.

Primary sources: [Commissioner's section 7 privacy-notice guide](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/A-Quick-Guide-to-PRIVACY-NOTICE.pdf), [current PDPA practice/circulars](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/personal-data-protection-general-practice/), and [DPO/other current FAQ](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/faq/).

Commercial-transaction/territorial scope, actual controller details, any applicable DPO/registration duty, overseas-transfer conditions, retention/security and breach operations remain to be assessed. The notice does not claim SC/BNM payment or digital-asset approval.

## Translation and review

The rollout uses checked-in catalogs for all five locales; no user's posts or profile data are sent for runtime translation. Existing reviewed translations were retained, missing public interface copy was drafted with a one-time translation aid, and common controls were reviewed/corrected manually. Placeholder/key coverage is automated. Legal translations and the Malay supplement still warrant review by qualified local counsel/native legal translators before representing the service as legally cleared.
